Scope: the landing page (`src/pages/Landing.jsx`), Persuade. First surface of a
system-wide world replacement; the app's remaining screens follow in later passes.

Audience: an adult ASL learner at a laptop deciding whether this is worth turning
their webcam on. Not a buyer. Secondary: an examiner who will check every claim.

Action: start practising, with no signup wall.
Proof: the gloss-to-English pair the engine really produces, on-device inference,
the eight signs named. No metric that the code cannot substantiate.

Constraints: all copy, routes and nav labels are kept. The blue/violet gradient,
badge pills, icon-tile card grids and centred layout are removed and must not
return anywhere in the new world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product's output is already a typographic form with centuries of
history — interlinear glossed text, morpheme-aligned, gloss over translation. The
page is a descriptive grammar of the product rather than a page about it. It
refuses the centred hero-plus-feature-triplet arrangement this category ships, and
it refuses that arrangement's predictable opposite, mono-on-black dev brutalism.

OWN-WORLD: The mimeographed descriptive grammar and the overprinted atlas plate,
not the field notebook. Two inks on cool pale duplicator stock (#E7E8E3): printing
black (#17181B) for text, one saturated bottle green (#1D5B3F) as the overprint
that owns rules, section marks, marginal annotations and whole bands. Cream,
parchment and lamplight are out — that is this world's softest rendition and the
one every model ships. Alegreya carries prose and headings for its true small
caps, which Leipzig glossing convention requires; Courier Prime carries every
gloss, notation and measurement, because a duplicated grammar's examples were
typed. Hairline rules, section marks, hanging example numbers, exact morpheme
alignment. No gradient, no pill, no icon tile, no card grid.

STORY: The visitor sees a numbered glossed example before any sales line and
understands within one viewport that this thing reads signing and returns real
English. They believe it because the example is the engine's actual output, not a
claim about it. They start practising without an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running head left, section mark right, hairline rule under both.
Example (1) leads at display scale on the left: gloss row in typed caps, morpheme
row beneath it, free translation in quotes below — all three hard-aligned on a
single left margin, never centred. One prose sentence states what the product does.
The primary action sits inline at the end of that reading line. A narrow right-hand
margin column carries the three facts as green marginal annotations in the
grammar's own voice, which is what replaces the removed badge pills.

FORM: Interlinear glossing / linguistic fieldwork notation. Position 1 on the
ordered grounded list, presented as IMPECCABLE'S PICK and chosen by the user over
the roll's assignment (index 3, chronophotography). Seed key 77c2d85d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
